Final list

NSE large, mid, and small cap shares. A name reaches this page only after every screen below has passed.

  1. 1

    Price and volume

    The 20-day exponential average must cross above the 50-day simple average. Volume on the move must be at least three times the prior 20-day average.

  2. 2

    Risk and momentum

    RSI must sit between 50 and 70. The daily range must be between 1% and 4% of the price, and the name must be higher than it was 20 sessions ago. A name that fails any screen is left out.

  3. 3

    Filings, news, and lawsuits

    The model reads those sources and either approves a buy or stands aside. A sell is shown as a call. It is not sent as an order.

Run Live places one Zerodha buy GTT for each approved buy. A share is sized to one lakh rupees.
